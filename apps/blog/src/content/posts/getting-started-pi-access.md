The Quantum Platform separates your personal research identity, your programmes and the resources authorized for each programme.

## Apply to lead a research programme

Create your account, verify email, enable MFA and complete your [research profile](https://quantum.nyameko.com/profile/). Visit [Apply as a PI](https://quantum.nyameko.com/programmes/apply/) to provide your institution, programme name and research description. The system assigns the official programme identifier on submission.

Review the application and membership status under [Research programmes](https://quantum.nyameko.com/programmes/). Submission is a request: review and explicit approval are necessary before programmes and their members can use protected research resources.

## Separate collaborators from compute authorization

Students and collaborators request membership under the approved programme; membership and resource entitlements have independent authorization checks. Do not share your personal SSH keys, VPN credentials or model access tokens with team members.

## From approved access to reproducible science

Use [Access & entitlements](https://quantum.nyameko.com/access/) to confirm allocation, then [Workbench](https://quantum.nyameko.com/workbench/) and [Runs](https://quantum.nyameko.com/runs/) to execute the first approved CPU workflow. Retain scheduler IDs, scientific reference results, source revision and manifests.
